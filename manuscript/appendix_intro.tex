\subsection*{Data sources and variable construction}
\textbf{CMS Provider of Services file} \newline
The Centers for Medicare \& Medicaid Services (CMS) Provider of Services (POS) file contains demographic and certification information for hospital and non-hospital facilities. The file is used in this study to determine potential hospital openings and closures. Facilities are filtered to short term acute care and critical access hospitals. Provided certification dates are used to identify potential hospital openings, while termination dates are used to identify potential closures. \newline

\noindent \textbf{Aggregation of socioeconomic and demographic characteristics} \newline
Socioeconomic and demographic characteristics were aggregated from zip code tabulation areas (ZCTAs) to hospital service areas (HSAs) by linking zip-to-zcta and zip-to-HSA crosswalks, counting ZCTAs once within each relevant HSA-year. Insurance coverage was weighted by the non-institutionalized population, unemployment by the civilian labor force, educational attainment by the population aged 25 years and older, and poverty by the population for whom poverty status was determined. Median household income and the social deprivation index were weighted by total population.
